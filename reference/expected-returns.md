## Key Rules (Never Break)

1. **3-year rule:** Never judge an equity fund on less than 3 years of data
2. **No performance chasing:** Last year's #1 fund is often next year's #5
3. **Switch TO index, not TO another active fund** — when active fails, passive is the answer
4. **Gold/Silver are insurance, not investments** — don't expect equity-like returns
5. **SIP continuity > fund selection** — stopping SIPs in a crash is the #1 wealth destroyer
6. **Tax awareness:** Every switch has STCG/LTCG implications. Only switch if post-tax math still favors it.

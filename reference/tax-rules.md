# Indian Investment Tax Rules — Quick Reference

> **Last verified:** May 2026 | **Regime:** New Tax Regime
> Update this file whenever SEBI/CBDT changes tax rules.

---

## Mutual Funds — Equity-Oriented (≥65% equity)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **LTCG threshold** | Holding > 12 months |
| **LTCG tax rate** | 12.5% |
| **LTCG annual exemption** | ₹1,25,000 |
| **STCG threshold** | Holding ≤ 12 months |
| **STCG tax rate** | 20% |
| **STT** | 0.001% on sell |
| **Grandfathering** | Gains up to Jan 31, 2018 exempt |

**Applies to:** UTI Nifty 50, PPFAS Flexi Cap, Motilal Mid150, Bandhan Small Cap, PGIM Mid, HDFC Flexi, Nippon Large, ICICI Large, all ELSS, SBI Tech, BOI Small Cap

---

## Mutual Funds — Non-Equity (Debt/Gold/Silver FoFs)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **LTCG threshold** | Holding > 24 months |
| **LTCG tax rate** | 12.5% |
| **LTCG exemption** | ₹1.25L (shared with equity LTCG) |
| **STCG threshold** | Holding ≤ 24 months |
| **STCG tax rate** | At income tax slab rate |
| **Indexation** | NOT available (removed from Apr 2025) |

**Applies to:** SBI Gold ETF FoF, Nippon Silver ETF FoF

## Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Maturity (8 years)** | **Tax-free** — no capital gains tax |
| **Early redemption (after 5Y)** | Taxed as LTCG at 12.5% |
| **Secondary market sale** | LTCG 12.5% (>12 months) / STCG at slab (<12 months) |
| **Annual interest (2.5%)** | Taxed at income slab rate |

**Action:** Always hold to maturity for tax-free exit.

---

## Employee Provident Fund (EPF)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Interest rate** | 8.25% (FY 2025-26) |
| **Tax on interest** | Tax-free up to ₹2.5L/year contribution |
| **Withdrawal before 5Y** | Taxable at slab rate |
| **Withdrawal after 5Y** | Tax-free |

---

## ELSS Funds

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Lock-in** | 3 years from each SIP installment |
| **80C deduction** | ❌ NOT available in New Tax Regime |
| **Post lock-in** | Treated as equity MF (12.5% LTCG) |

**Action:** No tax benefit in new regime. Redeem as unlocked, move to target allocation.

## New Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025-26)

| Income Slab | Tax Rate |
|-------------|----------|
| Up to ₹4,00,000 | Nil |
| ₹4,00,001 – ₹8,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹8,00,001 – ₹12,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹12,00,001 – ₹16,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹16,00,001 – ₹20,00,000 | 20% |
| ₹20,00,001 – ₹24,00,000 | 25% |
| Above ₹24,00,000 | 30% |

**Standard Deduction:** ₹75,000
**Rebate u/s 87A:** Full tax rebate if taxable income ≤ ₹12,00,000

## Annual Tax Optimization Calendar

| Month | Action | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **January** | Book ₹1.25L LTCG | Use full annual exemption before March FY-end |
| **January** | Immediately re-buy same units | Reset cost basis (no wash-sale rule in India) |
| **March** | Verify total LTCG < ₹1.25L | Avoid unexpected tax bill |
| **April** | Step-up SIPs by 10% | New FY starts |
| **June-Aug** | Redeem unlocked ELSS units | 3Y lock-in completed, move to target funds |

---

## Key Rules for Rebalancing Decisions

1. **Never sell equity before 12 months** — STCG at 20% vs LTCG at 12.5%
2. **Annual LTCG harvesting** — Sell and rebuy ₹1.25L gains in January
3. **STP units inherit purchase date** — Each STP installment has its own 12-month clock
4. **Debt FoF gains taxed at slab** if held <24 months — avoid short-term exits on Gold/Silver FoF
5. **No wash-sale rule in India** — Can sell and immediately rebuy for tax harvesting

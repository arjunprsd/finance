**Applicable:** FY 2025-26 & FY 2026-27 | **Regime:** New Tax Regime (Selected)

## 1. New Tax Regime Slabs (Default from FY 2024-25)

| Income Slab         | Tax Rate | Cumulative Tax |
|---------------------|----------|----------------|
| Up to ₹4,00,000    | 0%       | ₹0             |
| ₹4,00,001 – ₹8,00,000 | 5%   | ₹20,000        |
| ₹8,00,001 – ₹12,00,000| 10%  | ₹60,000        |
| ₹12,00,001 – ₹16,00,000| 15% | ₹1,20,000      |
| ₹16,00,001 – ₹20,00,000| 20% | ₹2,00,000      |
| ₹20,00,001 – ₹24,00,000| 25% | ₹3,00,000      |
| Above ₹24,00,000   | 30%      | —              |

**+ 4% Health & Education Cess on total tax**

### Key Benefits Under New Regime:
- Standard Deduction: ₹75,000
- Zero tax up to ₹12 lakh income (₹12.75L with std deduction) via Section 87A rebate
- Employer NPS contribution deduction up to 14% of salary (80CCD(2))

### What You CANNOT Claim Under New Regime:
- ❌ Section 80C (₹1.5L) — ELSS, PPF, LIC, etc.
- ❌ Section 80D — Health insurance premium
- ❌ HRA Exemption
- ❌ Home Loan Interest (Section 24b for self-occupied)
- ❌ NPS 80CCD(1B) — Additional ₹50K
- ❌ Leave Travel Allowance

## 2. Capital Gains Tax on Mutual Funds

### A. Equity Mutual Funds (≥65% equity)
*Includes: Large-cap, Mid-cap, Small-cap, Flexi-cap, ELSS, Equity Hybrid*

| Type | Holding Period | Tax Rate | Exemption |
|------|---------------|----------|-----------|
| **LTCG** | > 12 months | **12.5%** | **₹1.25 lakh/year exempt** |
| **STCG** | ≤ 12 months | **20%** flat | None |

**Important Notes:**
- ₹1.25L LTCG exemption is ANNUAL across all equity instruments combined
- No indexation benefit on equity
- Section 87A rebate NOT available on capital gains
- Surcharge applicable on high incomes

**Tax Planning Example:**
```
You redeem equity MFs and realize ₹3,00,000 LTCG in a year:

  First ₹1,25,000  → TAX FREE
  Next  ₹1,75,000  × 12.5% = ₹21,875
  + 4% Cess        = ₹875
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Total Tax         = ₹22,750

  Effective rate on ₹3L LTCG = 7.6% (not 12.5%)
```

**SIP Tax Harvesting Strategy:**
- Each SIP installment has its own 12-month holding period
- Redeem older units (>12 months) up to ₹1.25L LTCG each year = TAX FREE
- Reinvest the proceeds immediately → resets cost basis higher
- Best done in January-March each year

### B. Debt Mutual Funds (<35% equity)
*Includes: Liquid, Short Duration, Corporate Bond, Gilt, Gold Funds, International Funds*

| Type | Holding Period | Tax Rate | Indexation |
|------|---------------|----------|------------|
| ALL gains | Any period | **Slab Rate** (up to 30%) | ❌ REMOVED |

**Since April 1, 2023:**
- No distinction between STCG and LTCG for debt funds
- All gains taxed at your income tax slab rate
- Indexation benefit completely removed
- Makes debt MFs tax-equivalent to FDs

**Implication:** For your tax bracket (~20-25%), debt MFs have NO tax advantage over bank FDs. Choose based on liquidity and convenience only.

### C. Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)

| Scenario | Tax Treatment |
|----------|---------------|
| Held to maturity (8 years) | **LTCG fully EXEMPT** |
| Sold on exchange before maturity | 12.5% LTCG (after 12 months) |
| Interest (2.5% per annum) | Taxable at slab rate |

→ **SGBs are the MOST tax-efficient gold investment** if held to maturity

### D. EPF

| Scenario | Tax Treatment |
|----------|---------------|
| Withdrawal after 5 years | **Fully exempt** |
| Interest up to ₹2.5L/year | Exempt |
| Interest above ₹2.5L/year | Taxable at slab |

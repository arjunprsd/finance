## The Step-Up Strategy — Why 10% Annual Increase

```
SALARY GROWTH ASSUMPTION: 12-15% per year (IT/tech industry avg)

If salary grows 15% and I increase SIP by only 10%:
→ My LIFESTYLE can improve by 5% each year (not a monk forever)
→ But investment RATE keeps growing faster than inflation

Year 1: ₹1.0L SIP on ₹2.0L income → 50% savings rate
Year 3: ₹1.21L SIP on ₹2.64L income → 46% savings rate (lifestyle improved)
Year 5: ₹1.46L SIP on ₹3.48L income → 42% savings rate (even more comfortable)

The magic: Even as savings RATE drops from 50% to 42%,
the AMOUNT grows from ₹1L to ₹1.46L — compounding on a larger base.

RULE: Every salary hike → AT LEAST 50% of increment goes to SIP increase.
Got ₹30K hike? → ₹15K more to SIPs, ₹15K to lifestyle.
```

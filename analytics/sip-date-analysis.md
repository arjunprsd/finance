## Why All on the 1st — Not Spread Across Dates

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Data says so** | Day 1 is ranked #1 for ALL 6 benchmarks over 5Y. No other date has this consistency. |
| **Spreading hurts Gold/Silver** | Day 24–26 is the WORST zone for Gold & Silver (costs 3–6pp). Spreading equity SIPs to late month would force commodities into their worst dates. |
| **Equity spread is negligible** | Best-to-worst spread for equity is <1.85pp over 5 YEARS — that's ₹90/month on ₹10K SIP. Not worth the complexity. |
| **Cash flow is clean** | Salary ₹2L on 1st → SIP ₹1L on 1st → ₹1L remaining for expenses. No mid-month crunch. |
| **Simplicity = compliance** | One date means zero chance of missing SIPs, zero admin overhead, zero "I'll optimize dates later" procrastination. |

## Cross-Asset Behavioural Summary

### The Two Regimes

| Metric | Equity (Nifty 50/500/Midcap/Smallcap) | Commodities (Gold/Silver) |
|--------|----------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Best date (long-term) | Day 1 | Day 1–2 |
| Worst date (consistent) | Day 15–18 | Day 20–28 |
| Best date stability | UNSTABLE (shifts by period) | STABLE (Day 1–2 from 6M onwards) |
| Spread (5Y) | 0.62–1.45pp (negligible) | 3.11–6.07pp (meaningful) |
| Date selection impact | Low — consistency matters more | Medium — early month reliably better |
| Behavioural driver | Salary-day buying pressure (5th–15th) | INR/USD month-end settlement effect |

### Why Patterns Differ

**Equity (worst mid-month):** Most Indian salaried investors receive salary between 1st–7th and set SIPs on 5th–15th. This concentrated buying pressure pushes mid-month prices up. SIPs on Day 1 (before the crowd) or Day 23–26 (after the pressure fades) get slightly better prices.

**Gold/Silver (worst late-month):** Gold is priced in USD internationally. Indian importers and banks settle dollar obligations at month-end, creating INR depreciation pressure. Since gold price in INR = international price × USD/INR rate, month-end INR weakness inflates gold prices in rupee terms. Buying early month avoids this.

### The Convergence on Day 1

Over 5 years, Day 1 ranks #1 for ALL 6 benchmarks. This isn't coincidence — it captures:
- Pre-salary-crowd equity prices (before the 5th–15th rush)
- Pre-month-end-settlement commodity prices (before INR weakens)
- Fresh monthly capital deployment (salary hits account, money works immediately)
- Zero idle cash days (no opportunity cost of money sitting in savings account)

## Methodology

### Data Collection
- **Source:** NSE India official data via NSE MCP API
- **Equity indices:** Daily closing price (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume, Turnover)
- **ETFs (GOLDBEES, SILVERBEES):** Daily closing price from equity price history
- **Period:** January 1, 2021 to May 16, 2026 (SILVERBEES from February 7, 2022)
- **Total data points:** ~1,330 per index (1,058 for Silver)

### SIP Simulation Logic
For each target SIP date (1–28):
1. For each calendar month in the dataset, find the first available trading day on or after the target date
2. If no trading day exists after the target date (rare), use the last trading day of that month
3. Invest ₹10,000 at that day's closing price → accumulate units
4. After all months, calculate final portfolio value using the last available closing price
5. Total return = (Final Value − Total Invested) / Total Invested × 100

### Period Analysis
- Each period (3M, 6M, 1Y, 1.5Y, 2Y, 3Y, 5Y) filters the dataset from `end_date - period_days` to `end_date`
- Same SIP simulation runs on the filtered dataset
- Returns are normalized (0–1 scale) per period for cross-period comparison
- Rankings are computed per period to track how Day 1's position shifts

### Limitations
- **Survivorship bias:** Only includes indices/ETFs that exist today
- **No exit load modelling:** SIP simulation assumes buy-and-hold, no interim redemptions
- **Single dataset:** 5.5 years covers one full market cycle (COVID crash → recovery → correction) but not multiple cycles
- **ETF vs NAV:** GOLDBEES/SILVERBEES prices include market premium/discount vs NAV; FoF NAV behaviour may differ slightly
- **Non-trading days:** When SIP date falls on a holiday, the next trading day is used (matches real broker behaviour)

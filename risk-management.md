## 3. Market Crash Playbook

### What to Do If Market Falls:

| Nifty Fall | Action | Rationale |
|------------|--------|-----------|
| -10% (correction) | Continue SIPs as normal | Normal volatility |
| -15 to -20% | Deploy 20% of emergency Tier 3 as lump sum | Opportunity, not crisis |
| -25 to -30% (crash) | Deploy 30-40% of emergency fund as lump sum into equity | Once-in-5-year opportunity |
| -30%+ (black swan) | Increase SIP by 50% for 3-6 months | Generational wealth opportunity |

**NEVER DO:**
- ❌ Stop SIPs during a crash
- ❌ Panic sell existing funds
- ❌ Move equity to FD during volatility
- ❌ Try to time the bottom

**ALWAYS:**
- ✅ Continue SIPs regardless of market level
- ✅ Have dry powder (emergency fund) for crashes
- ✅ Remember: SIPs work BEST in falling markets (rupee cost averaging)

## 7. Rebalancing Rules

### When to Rebalance:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Equity exceeds 80% of portfolio | Shift excess to debt fund |
| Equity drops below 60% of portfolio | Shift from debt to equity |
| Any single fund > 25% of equity portfolio | Trim and redistribute |
| Gold > 12% of portfolio | Stop gold SIP, redirect |
| Emergency fund drops below ₹4L | Pause equity SIP, rebuild |

### How to Rebalance (Tax-Efficient):
1. **Preferred:** Change SIP allocation (shift new money, don't sell)
2. **If needed:** Sell debt (slab rate anyway) → buy equity
3. **Last resort:** Sell equity (watch ₹1.25L LTCG limit)
